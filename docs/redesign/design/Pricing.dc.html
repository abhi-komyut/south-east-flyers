Exclusive delivery versus shared distribution

Many letterbox services run shared distribution. Your flyer is collated with other advertisers' material and delivered as one bundle, which lowers the flyer distribution cost per 1,000 because several businesses share the walk.

The trade-off is attention. In a bundle, your flyer competes with catalogues and other offers before the household even sees it. With exclusive delivery it arrives on its own, which matters most for businesses that need a phone call, such as a plumber, a real estate agent or a local clinic.

Working out how many flyers you need

Start with the letterboxes in your target area. Brighton, for example, has about 10,500 homes, so covering the whole suburb costs about $2,100 (see flyer distribution in Brighton).

Most businesses don't need a whole suburb on day one. A plumber might cover 2,500 homes around their base; an agent might drop 1,000 flyers around a new listing. Tell us the area and we will confirm the leaflet drop cost and letterbox count in your quote.

Ways to get more from your budget

  • Test with 1,000 flyers in one area, track the calls, then expand to the streets that respond.
  • Put one clear offer and your phone number on the front. A busy flyer costs the same to deliver as a clear one.
  • Time the drop to demand. Heating and roofing flyers work before winter; cooling and pool services before summer.
  • Repeat in the streets that respond. A second drop builds recognition, and the GPS reports let you compare calls run by run.
