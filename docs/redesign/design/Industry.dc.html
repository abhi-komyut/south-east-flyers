Why agents still drop flyers

The people most likely to sell next are the neighbours of the house you just listed. They watch the board go up, check the price and wonder what their own place is worth.

A real estate letterbox drop in those streets answers that question with your name on it. Real estate flyer distribution also shows vendors that the marketing is happening. A GPS map of every street covered is easier to put in a vendor report than an impression count.

One listing, four drops

A typical campaign uses the same streets four times, each with a different message.

  1. Listing week

    Just Listed

    Photo, key features and open home times to the surrounding streets.

  2. Before the first open

    Invitation

    A reminder drop two or three days out, timed with our 48-hour start.

  3. Auction week

    Auction notice

    Date, time and a line inviting neighbours to come along.

  4. After the result

    Just Sold

    The result and an appraisal offer, while the street is still talking about it.

Just Sold and appraisal flyers

Just sold flyers are often the most useful drop for an agent's pipeline. They prove you can sell in that street, and they arrive just as neighbours are recalculating what their home might be worth.

  • Lead with the result and the street, not your headshot.
  • Offer a free appraisal with a direct mobile number.
  • Repeat appraisal flyers to your farm area every four to six weeks so your name stays familiar.

Real estate letterbox drop FAQs

How many flyers does a Just Listed drop need?
Most agents cover the few streets immediately around the property. Our minimum is 1,000 flyers ($200), which covers those streets with room to extend into your wider farm area.
Which agencies do you deliver for?
We distribute for agencies including Ray White, McGrath and Belle Property, running Just Listed, Open Home, Auction and Sold campaigns across their local areas.
Can I show my vendor proof the flyers were delivered?
Yes. Every run is GPS-tracked in Strava, and you get a map of the streets covered and the dates. You can forward it straight to the vendor.
How quickly can a Just Listed campaign start?
Urgent campaigns can start within 48 hours of your flyers arriving, which suits the tight timeline before a first open home. Standard campaigns start within 3 to 5 business days.
Can you focus on houses and skip apartment blocks?
Yes. Tell us, and we will plan the route around houses and townhouses where that is practical, which usually suits a farming campaign better than full suburb coverage. The GPS report shows exactly what was covered.
