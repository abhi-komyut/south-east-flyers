Brighton at a glance

Brighton is one of the most established suburbs we cover: large period and post-war homes, wide tree-lined streets, and owners who have often lived in the same house for decades. Its shops sit along Church Street and Bay Street, and the Sandringham line runs down the middle of the suburb.

For a letterbox drop that means about 10,500 homes, most of them houses with street letterboxes, and households that read what arrives rather than skimming it.

Long-term owners, big decisions

Households that have owned their home for decades make the large, considered purchases: selling and downsizing, renovating a kitchen, replacing a roof, choosing a new dentist. Those decisions take weeks, and a flyer that stays on the bench gets looked at more than once.

That suits businesses with a high-value job and a long decision, such as real estate agents running appraisal campaigns, roofers, renovators and health practices. Repeat drops to the same streets work better here than one big blast.

Mostly houses, so coverage is high

Most Brighton homes are houses and townhouses with letterboxes on the street, so a drop reaches nearly every door on the route. In suburbs with more apartment towers, locked mail rooms cut that number down.

Shops and cafes around Church Street and Bay Street draw from the blocks within walking distance, so a 1,000 to 2,500 flyer drop around the strip is a sensible first test. Trades and agents usually pick the streets nearest their base or their listing.

Timing a Brighton drop around the property market

Melbourne's busiest selling season runs from September to early December, with a smaller peak in autumn. Brighton agents push appraisal flyers in the weeks before each season, and renovators, painters and landscapers follow, because owners getting ready to sell want the house looking its best.

If your business benefits from that cycle, book your Brighton drops for late winter and late summer, when owners are planning rather than already in the middle of a campaign.

Do Brighton apartments get flyers?
Only where the letterboxes can be reached from the street. Blocks with locked mail rooms are skipped, and the GPS report shows which ones so you know your real coverage before the next run.
Where in Brighton should a first drop go?
Shops and cafes usually start with the streets around Church Street or Bay Street. Agents start with the blocks around a listing. Trades start closest to their base.
