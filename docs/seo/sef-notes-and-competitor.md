## Competitor: letterboxdistributors.com.au

| Area | What they do | Gap / how SEF beats it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melbourne page | Title "Letterbox Drops & Flyer Distribution Melbourne"; H1 "Leaflet & Flyer Distribution Melbourne"; Campbellfield depot (north); 1.25M homes, 320 suburbs | Position SEF as the South East specialist; lead with GPS proof per drop, which they don't offer |
| Region & suburb pages | 8 region pages incl. /flyer-distribution-melbourne-south-east/; 15 suburb pages, mostly north/west. SE only: Glen Waverley, Noble Park, Mount Waverley, Rowville | 54 of SEF's 56 key suburbs have no suburb page from them (they cover only Glen Waverley and Mount Waverley) |
| Home counts | Lists homes per suburb with postcode (e.g. Glen Waverley 3150: 13,984) | Add home counts + postcode to every SEF suburb page; it's useful and earns trust |
| Industry pages | Real estate, restaurants/menus, tradies (plumbers, electricians, builders, cleaners, gardeners, pest control, AC, gutters, fencing), schools & childcare | They have no pet groomer, vet, dental, medical or roofing pages — SEF owns those |
| Service pages | SME flyers, targeted distribution, pamphlet delivery, catalogue delivery, flyer/catalogue/brochure printing | Add catalogue + pamphlet pages; printing page only if SEF can fulfil |
| Pricing | Minimum 10,000 homes per order. Cost page has FAQs but no figures | SEF minimum is 1,000 flyers — big hook for small businesses. SEF's own confirmed price is $200 per 1,000, exclusive (not bundled) — publish that on the cost page |
| Distribution model | Shared distribution (flyers bundled); follows DSB Code of Practice | SEF's "zero bundling" (solo delivery) is a differentiator — say it in every title/H2 |
| Quote promise | "Firm quote within 6 hours" | Match or beat: "quote in 2 hours" plus click-to-call |
| FAQs | Only on cost page | Put suburb- and industry-specific FAQ schema on every new page |
| Keywords they target | letterbox drop(s), letterbox distribution, flyer distribution, leaflet distribution, pamphlet delivery, catalogue delivery, targeted letterbox drops by suburb/postcode, letterbox drop cost | Use "flyer distribution" + "letterbox drops" together in titles, as they do; add "GPS-tracked" and "solo delivery" as modifiers |

**Note on the quote-promise suggestion:** "quote in 2 hours" is a competitive suggestion in this sheet, not a confirmed SEF commitment. The build-context doc still says "quote within 24 hours." Don't change the published turnaround claim without Abhi confirming which one is accurate.
