### Strategic reads
- **"letterbox drops" (390) beats "letterbox distribution" (210)** — both are woven into the letterbox page + Melbourne page.
- **"flyer distribution melbourne" = "leaflet distribution melbourne" (170 each)** — same intent; "leaflet" is used as a secondary term on the Melbourne page.
- **"real estate letterbox drops" (30) > "real estate flyer distribution" (10)** — the real estate page leads with letterbox-drop language.
- **Targeted & GPS pages have ZERO head-term demand** — kept as trust/conversion + internal-linking pages, not primary organic targets. They rank for long-tail and support the cluster; don't over-invest in their exact-match head terms.
